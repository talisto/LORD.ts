# Violet's Cottage

**A character interaction IGM for Legend of the Red Dragon**

## Overview

Violet's Cottage lets players romp through the forest with Violet's family. The IGM features gender-specific content, gossip systems, resource management, and interactions with Violet and her cook at the cottage.

### Features

- **Cottage Interior**: Visit Violet's cottage and interact with characters
- **Porch**: Explore the cottage porch area
- **Gender-Specific Content**: Additional options for both male and female players, with expanded female-oriented content in later versions
- **Gossip**: Female players can gossip with other female visitors
- **Resource Management**: Gems, experience, and health interactions
- **ANSI Art**: Original ANSI art by Solus
