# The Warrior's Graveyard

**A graveyard exploration IGM for Legend of the Red Dragon**

## Overview

The Warrior's Graveyard is a graveyard-themed IGM where players can rob graves for treasure, discover hidden ghost characters with unique challenges, buy mysterious potions, and interact with colorful NPCs.

### Features

- **Grave Robbing**: Search newly dug graves for gold and gems
- **Hidden Ghosts**: Discover 4 secret ghost characters (Jim Bob Jones, George Price, Tanya Travis, Mike the Knife) with challenges and rewards based on player stats and gender
- **Shopping**: Buy enchanted Lemon-Aide from a juice seller with mysterious effects
- **NPC Interactions**: Talk to the Grave Digger and Helga the old hag for potions and games
