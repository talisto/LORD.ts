# Aragorn's Timer

**A number-guessing game IGM for Legend of the Red Dragon**

## Overview

Aragorn's Timer is a once-per-day IGM where players enter Aragorn's Cave and play a timed number-guessing game. Players wager gold and have 20 seconds and 15 tries to guess a number from 1 to 1000. A correct guess earns 150% of the wager back; failure means losing it all.
