# The Outhouse

**A quick daily IGM for Legend of the Red Dragon**

## Overview

The Outhouse is a simple, quick daily IGM where players visit the outhouse and can trade forest fights for a chance at stat boosts. Players may gain or lose charm, defense, or strength depending on their choices and random outcomes.

### Features

- Trade 1 Forest Fight for a chance at stat boosts (Charm, Defense, or Strength)
- Find extra loot
- Gain or lose charm based on random encounters
- Quick daily interaction
