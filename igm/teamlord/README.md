# TeamLord

A team-based warfare and management IGM for Legend of the Red Dragon.

## Overview

TeamLord adds persistent teams to LORD. Players can found or join a team, manage a shared house and treasury, train a doorguard dragon, revive fallen team mates, sleep more safely in the team house, and launch attacks or invasions against rival teams.

### Features

- **Team Creation and Joining**: Create a new team house or join an existing team with a password
- **Team Treasury**: Deposit and withdraw shared funds from the team safe
- **Doorguard Dragon**: Buy and train a house dragon in strength and endurance to defend sleeping team members
- **Team Warfare**: Attack rival teams and invade team houses for combat and theft opportunities
- **Team House**: Stay the night in the team house instead of the inn
- **Party System**: Head to the inn, grab a date, or throw a party for various stat and gold effects
- **Team Management**: Change the team password, kick members, display teams, list team mates, and send team-wide messages
- **Revival System**: Spend gold to revive dead team mates
- **Healers and Banking**: Use dedicated healing and banking menus inside the IGM
- **Multinode Support**: Designed to keep team state synchronized when multiple players are in the IGM at the same time
